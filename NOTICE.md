Twitter Hide Ads is an independent Xposed module and is not affiliated with X Corp., Twitter, Vector, libxposed or DexKit.

The module uses one adaptive upstream Video Tab dataset hook alongside the adaptive post render boundaries. Internal host-app structures may change without notice; all adaptive paths fail open when confidence or compatibility is insufficient.

The launcher icon (`docs/icon.svg`, `app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml`) is original vector artwork for this project; the X mark is a trademark of X Corp. and is used only to identify the app the module targets.
